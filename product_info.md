## 1. GoodFills Brand / Product Philosophy

According to page 2:

-   GoodFills products are made using an old traditional method
    involving washing, soaking, sun-drying, and sprouting pulses and
    grains.
-   Products are made to order, with customer demands and customization
    kept in mind.
-   Products are not made in mass quantities and stored for long
    durations.
-   Products are intended for daily use by family members.
-   The brand provides skincare, healthy eating, and nutrition products
    inspired by traditional homemade preparation.
-   The catalog states that products are not factory-made with harsh
    chemicals or under unhygienic conditions.
-   Each product is carefully sealed with the customer in mind.
-   Pricing is described as competitive compared with other brands, with
    customers paying for customization based on a baby's, child's, or
    individual's skin needs.
